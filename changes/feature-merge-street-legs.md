# feature/merge-street-legs

- Neighbouring street legs of the same kind are one leg now. The router splits a trip at every station: a tram ride ending at one post of a stop, a 29 m transfer walk to the next post and the street walk to the destination used to come as `walk, tram, walk, walk`; now it is `walk, tram, walk`. The same for neighbouring own-bike legs.
- `backend/app/routing/merge.py` (`merge_street_legs`), used in `RealRouteEngine._plan_option`. The route itself does not change: the points are the same and in the same order (the shared point once), the minutes and metres of the merged leg are the sums of its parts, so the totals of the option are unchanged; `fromId` is the first leg's, `toId` the last leg's, segments / elevators / steps are joined in order, the private street totals (walked / ridden metres, waits) are carried over. The adapter `legs_from_router` is not touched.
- Not joined: any ride, legs of different modes, rented `veturilo` legs (a second rental is another ride), a leg that ends at an intermediate stop of a multi-stop trip.
- Checked on the running backend: 8 pairs of points, 27 options recorded before and after. Every option field (id, title, minutes, metres, walk / bike distance, waits, transfers, tags, times) is equal; 4 runs of walks were joined and in each the mode, minutes, metres, names, ids, start / end, path points and segments equal the parts put together. No neighbouring walk legs are left. Unit tests: `backend/tests/test_merge_legs.py` (14 cases). The own-bike and Veturilo cases are covered by unit tests only (the bike matrices are not built on this machine).
- Assumption: minutes of a merged leg are the sum of the rounded minutes of its parts (so the sum of the legs of an option stays exactly as before) rather than one rounding of the exact seconds.
- Frontend: nothing to change; it already draws whatever legs it gets (and the "walk" chip appears once).
